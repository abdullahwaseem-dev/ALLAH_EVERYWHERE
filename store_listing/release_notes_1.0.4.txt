English:

NEW: Share a Reminder
Tell us how you're feeling - sad, anxious, grateful, grieving, being tested and more - and get Quran ayat and authentic Hadith that speak to it. Turn any of them into a beautiful image with your name and photo, choose from 24 light and dark backgrounds, and share it straight to your WhatsApp Status or Instagram Story and Post.

IMPROVEMENTS
- Hadith load much faster, and chapters you have opened are available offline
- New, easier-to-read Arabic and Urdu fonts for Hadith
- Seerat-un-Nabi and Tib-e-Nabwi now switch to your chosen language (8 languages)
- The Adhan now plays for prayer notifications on iPhone
- Share any Hadith as an image from the Hadith reader

FIXES
- "Share the App" now works on iPhone
- "Rate the App" now opens the App Store
- Bookmarks save reliably, even when you are offline
- Other bug fixes and performance improvements
